## Duplicate verdict

Not a duplicate. No extract on this base cites this title or this symposium. It is not the 2003 mullite paper (`shornikov-2003-mullite-thermodynamics`).

## What was admitted

No scored observations. MinerU found no table. Partial pressures are not tabulated. Activities and ΔfG/RT are figures only.

x*(SiO2) = 10.94±0.18 mol% at 2100 K is a composition anchor for a*(i), stored as context, not as an activity.

## Standard state

SiO2, equation (5): the integral starts at x(SiO2)=1. The cell samples stop at 60 mol% SiO2, so this is not a pure oxide measured in the cell. The paper does not state solid or liquid. Expected migration phase: unknown. The pure-oxide-at-T ruling was not used.

CaO, equation (6), and TiO2, equation (7): a*(i) is the activity of component i in CaTiO3 crystallized at x*. That is a compound at x*, which the ruling leaves unknown. No melting point was applied.

CaTiO3 and CaTiSiO5: the paper does not state a reference. Unknown.

Fig. 2 caption does not state the formation reference. Unknown. Series 1 is reference [13] (the already extracted 2001 SiO2–MgAl2O4 paper) at 2050 K. Series 2 is reference [14] at 1933 K. Neither was read as this paper’s data.
