## What was admitted

No observations. MinerU found no table. The 600 dpi page names CaAlO, CaSiO3, MgAlO, AlSiO, and CaTiO3, says they do not exceed 1% of the total gas concentration over 1500–2500 K, and describes partial-pressure trends without a composition. The 1% bound and the "more than an order of magnitude" statement have no composition, so they stay context. Predominant gases Ca, Mg, Al, AlO, TiO, and SiO are named the same way.
