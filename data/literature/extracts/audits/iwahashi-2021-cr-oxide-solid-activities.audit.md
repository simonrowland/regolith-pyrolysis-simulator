# Iwahashi et al. (2021) extraction audit

## Source and read order

The source is the born-digital ISIJ International paper, 61(5), 1404–1411, DOI 10.2355/isijinternational.ISIJINT-2020-752. Transcription began with MinerU HTML table blocks and Markdown, then the exact `pdftotext-layout.txt` layer. The MinerU `<details>` chart blocks were ignored. PDF renders were used only for the table layout assignments, the five Table 3 QA mismatch flags, the Eq. 14 superscript marker, and equation values/uncertainties where the machine text needs a visual check.

## Printed row counts and dispositions

- Table 1: 3 data rows. All nine bulk composition cells agree between MinerU and pdftotext; page 4 render confirms the row and column layout. The phase assemblage results are retained as three typed-refusal context records because there is no exact scored phase-assemblage observation type/quantity. Their numeric composition and experiment conditions remain preserved.
- Table 2: 24 data rows, 12 paired runs. All machine-read numeric cells agree; page 4 render confirms row-group layout and the X_Cu header. The prose and Eq. 15 define the measured composition as chromium mole fraction X_Cr in molten copper. The printed X_Cu header is preserved with the interpretation note on each row. Table 2 rows carry measured equilibrium X_Cr, initial X_Cr, gas pH2 and pCO2, log(P_O2/atm), time, temperature, total pressure, and Cu-Cr composition.
- Table 3: 8 phase assemblages × 3 component activities = 24 values. QA flagged five numeric disagreements with the exact text layer; every activity cell was confirmed against the PDF page 7 render. The authors calculate these activities from standard Gibbs energy changes for Reactions 17, 30, 32, 36, 38 and 40. Each row is `measured_reduced` and carries `derivation.raw_inputs.state: not_printed` with a page 7/Table 3 locator because the cell-specific combination of reaction energies is not printed.
- Equations: Eq. 14 K(13)=+23.94 is attributed to Turkdogan [2] and preserved as a quoted input; its superscript 2 reference marker was checked on the page 4 render. Eq. 24 log gamma_Cr=1.54 is a measured reduction from nine Table 2 rows below X_Cr=10^-2 and K(13); its equation and nested Eq. 24 condition locators are on PDF page 5. Eqs. 26 and 27 are measured reductions from their respective eight and six Table 2 rows, Eq. 24 and K(13), using Eq. 25; uncertainties ±0.09 and ±0.08 are retained with confidence level `not_stated`.

## Evidence and conditions

Table 2 Cr-in-liquid-Cu equilibrium compositions are `measured_direct` (ICP analysis of alloy samples). Eq. 24, Eqs. 26–27 and the authors' Table 3 activity calculations are `measured_reduced`; Table 3 records the cell-specific, unprinted reaction-energy combinations as located raw inputs. K(13) remains `quoted_attributed` to Turkdogan [2]. Table 1 assemblages are direct XRD results, but remain context because the closed observation and quantity vocabularies do not encode phase-set membership. The schema check consulted `tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::TYPE_QUANTITY`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::PHASE_BASIS_TOKENS`, and `simulator/battery/enums.py::PhaseRole`; the `equilibrium_phase_composition` precedent in park-2021 represents a component amount in a phase, not a multiphase assemblage.

The paper explicitly says the gas phase is at one atmospheric pressure (PDF p. 3); this printed condition maps to 101325 Pa. The phase-relation series prints initial pH2=2.50×10^-1 atm, pCO2=1.67×10^-3 atm and calculated pO2=1.02×10^-15 atm. Table 2 prints absolute log(P_O2/atm) row values and initial gas partial pressures. A declared 1 573 K activity-equilibration series carries the printed total pressure through the existing experiment-to-identity path for aggregate Eq. 24/26/27 results. Those fits span multiple oxygen potentials and compositions, so their single-point composition/fO2 remain explicitly unknown; the series gas field also stays unknown because its gas pressures vary by run. Table 3 calculated assemblage activities and K(13) carry unknown total pressure, composition and absolute fO2. No gas-flow rate was treated as pressure.
