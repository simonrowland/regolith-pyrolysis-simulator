# Hayashi et al. 2025 extraction audit

## Coverage and cell audit

Printed Table 1 has 14 rows: A1–A5, B1–B2, C1–C3, D1–D2, and E1–E2. Table 1(a) reports pO2 for all 14. Table 1(b) reports four mol% composition cells for all 14. Table 1(c) reports CFe(Pt) for 11 rows; A1–A3 contain dashes. Table 1(d) and 1(e) each report two values for 11 rows, for 44 activity and coefficient values. Table 1(a) has Mössbauer fractions for A1–A5, C1, and E1 only (seven samples). The prose says “7 samples out of 13 samples,” while the printed table contains 14 rows; the extract follows the table and preserves the seven sample-specific measured rows.

The companion `hayashi-2025-ca-fe-silicate-redox-activities.cells.csv` audits every stored numeric Table 1 cell: 146 in total (14 pO2, 21 valence cells, 56 composition cells, 11 CFe(Pt) cells, and 44 activities / coefficients). All 14 pO2 exponent cells were flagged LaTeX and confirmed from the page 3 render (method b). The other 132 stored cells agree in MinerU and pdftotext (method a); the page 3 render confirms the table's row and column assignment. The printed Table 1(c) C/S column has 14 additional numeric cells and is outside this redox/activity task. No values in `<details>` blocks or plotted figures were used.

The render confirmed the printed Table 1 subparts (a)–(e). Extract row locators use the printed labels `1(a)` through `1(e)` and the absolute PDF page 3. The mapping file's `table_index: 1` is only the draft-tool key for the single MinerU table block; it is not used as an extract locator. The exact CLI compare reports 347 locator errors because `tools/mineru_table_draft.py::_normalize_table_label` compares a parenthetical subtable label such as `1(a)` against the page caption's `1`, and `tools/mineru_table_draft.py::_phase_matches_column` does not accept the canonical `glass` or `l` phase tokens. For numeric matching only, a temporary copy normalized the table labels to `1` and omitted the phase tokens; it matched all 65 observation values in `hayashi-2025-ca-fe-silicate-redox-activities.compare-values.csv`. The committed extract retains its exact printed subtable locators and phases. The two comparison files record the direct tool result and the row/column value match after those comparator-only normalizations.

## Conditions, phase, and evidence

The experiment prose on page 8 fixes T at 1573 K. Each sample's pO2 is printed in atm in Table 1(a), retained as printed in `values.oxygen_partial_pressure`, and converted to Pa only for `point_conditions.fO2_Pa` and identity mapping. CO–CO2 runs report a total flow of 100 mL min-1; the per-sample CO/CO2 flow ratios are not printed. Ar–O2 runs are identified for D2, E1, and E2; no Ar/O2 ratio or total flow is stated. No experimental total pressure is printed. Every row records the unknown total pressure rather than assuming a pressure from the gas flow or atmosphere. A1–A5 melting durations (12, 18, 24, 36, and 48 h) are from page 2. The general 36 h equilibration and the 72 h Ar–O2 runs D2, E1, and E2 are from page 8.

The seven Mössbauer rows represent quenched glass measured spectroscopically at room temperature; their experimental run temperature remains 1573 K. The source states that samples were quenched into iced water and that X-ray diffraction confirmed all samples were glassy, but it does not claim that quenching preserves or alters the reported valence fractions. The paper prints Fe2+/Fetotal, Fe3+(oct)/Fetotal, and Fe3+(tetr)/Fetotal percentages, not a combined Fe3+/Fe2+ ratio. Fe2+/Fetotal is represented as a valence fraction. The two coordination-site fractions per measured sample remain typed refusals: `simulator/battery/identity.py::ValenceBasis` has no coordination-site field; `simulator/battery/enums.py::PHASE_BASIS_TOKENS` is a closed composition-basis set and `simulator/battery/enums.py::PhaseRole` identifies material phases, not Fe coordination sites; `simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES` and `TYPE_QUANTITY` support valence ratios but provide no exact site-fraction identity; and `tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES` is closed. `equilibrium_phase_composition` represents composition by phase and is not this site-specific valence fraction. The 14 refused row ids are pinned by the focused test.

The Table 1(a) Mössbauer percentages are this paper's own fits calculated from relative absorption areas and are `measured_reduced`; the underlying Figure 1 spectra are not digitized, but that does not change the origin of the reported measurements. The 14 octahedral and tetrahedral site-fraction rows remain typed refusals because the schema has no exact identity for those fractions. Table 1(c) CFe(Pt) values are this paper's own ZAF EPMA measurements reduced from X-ray intensities using Eq. (4), so their 11 context rows are `measured_reduced` even though the raw intensities and CFe(slag) inputs are not tabulated. The Table 1(b) compositions for B1, B2, C2, C3, D1, D2, and E2 are `model_derived`: page 4 states that they were estimated using Eqs. (1)–(3). The compositions for A4, A5, C1, and E1 remain `measured_direct`; printed values in the two columns are retained independently, without editing one to equal another. The Table 1(d) activities use the Fe-Pt activity-coefficient relation and Eqs. (13)–(14), and are `model_derived`; each points to its same-sample CFe(Pt) input context. The Table 1(e) coefficients use the stored matching activity plus the same-sample Table 1(b) composition context via gamma_i = a_i/x_i, and remain `model_derived`. The extract preserves the paper's FeO1.5 formula and Raoultian solid reference for that activity; it does not convert to an Fe2O3 basis.

Composition cells remain exactly as printed in mol% on `values.composition_as_published`. Their nominal sums are not normalized; for example, sample E2 sums to 100.5 mol%. `FeO1.5` is not a parseable species token, and the paper does not print an Fe2O3 amount, so each row's `point_conditions.composition` is a typed unknown whose reason names `FeO1.5`. The printed mol% map is not rewritten. Figures 1–3 and 6–7 remain figure-read candidates and were not digitized. The source's CO–CO2 flow ratio relation is retained in the method description, but the individual ratios are not calculated because the per-gas flows are absent.

## Isolated migration

The real migrator emits 65 observations, 22 context rows, and one experiment; it reports zero hard issues. Fourteen observations (the seven samples' octahedral and tetrahedral Fe3+ fractions) are unavailable after the exact schema-gap refusals. The remaining 51 observations have typed quantities and phases. The focused migration test asserts that the quantity-unknown set is exactly those 14 rows, no observation has an unknown phase, printed T and fO2 reach every observation, the composition point condition and identity are the typed unknown that names `FeO1.5`, and the identity's total pressure stays unknown. It also pins the required identity axes for A1, A4, and E2, including the printed-percentage conversion and pO2-to-Pa mapping.

The expected battery-store delta, without regeneration, is 65 observations (51 typed values and 14 typed-unavailable refusals), 22 context rows, and one experiment. On every observation, identity.composition and point_conditions.composition are the typed unknown that names FeO1.5; the printed mol% stays on values.composition_as_published and on the composition context rows. The store was not regenerated.
