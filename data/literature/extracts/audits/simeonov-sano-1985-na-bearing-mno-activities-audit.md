## Conditions and derived quantities

Table 1 states 1300 °C, CO atmosphere, and carbon saturation. It does not print a pressure for these distribution runs or final row-specific slag/metal compositions. The paper's separate 1 atm CO statement is in its activity-coefficient experiment description and was not carried onto Table 1. Figure 6 itself annotates the Na2O series with P_CO = 1 atm and lists MnO ≈1.5–3.5 wt%, CaF2 ≈30 wt%, and SiO2 ≈11.5 wt%; Section II.1 gives carbon-saturated iron with 5.5–6.5 wt% Mn. These series-level statements are retained on the typed-refused figure context, without inventing a CaO balance or per-run metal assays. Numeric final compositions, Na2O activity, and MnO activity coefficient for the Na2O-bearing series are absent from tables; plotted values remain deferred. Figure 7/8 (temperature-dependent distribution/capacity), Figures 9–10 (MnO activity coefficient), and Figure 11 (cross-system comparison including Na2O slags) are useful figure-read candidates.

The distribution values are the paper's own reduced equilibrium results (`measured_reduced`): the printed relation is `(MnO)/[Mn]`, while the row-level slag and metal assay inputs are not printed. The Mn formula routes the ratio to the redox rail. The high-temperature equilibrium melt is liquid (`phase: l`); slag and metal remain the phase labels. Equation (8) defines the manganous capacity, with `a_Mn(l)` referenced to liquid manganese. Since no existing schema token represents manganous capacity, Table 1's four `log C_Mn2+` values are typed-refused context with the printed relation and reference state. Section III.1 reports γ°_Mn = 0.510 at 1300 °C, calculated using the Fig. 2 slope 1.07 and Hultgren and Desai's quoted γ_Mn in copper of 0.544; this value is stored as the paper's measured-reduced result. Equation (5), the −53 kcal/mol reaction heat, the +31 kcal/mol relative partial heat of solution, and the initial BOF analysis are retained as typed context. The initial BOF analysis is not assigned to the Table 1 row identities. The Na2O text/figure does not resolve complete per-run inputs, so those statements are not admitted as observations. No unprinted pressure, fO2, composition, or metal assay was supplied.
