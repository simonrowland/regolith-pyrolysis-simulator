# Simeonov & Sano (1985) extraction audit

## Table 1

MinerU found one printed table: Table 1, PDF page 5 (journal page 1120), six columns, four data rows. The table reports four manganese distribution values in column `L_Mn` and four manganous capacities in column `log C_Mn2+`; row labels are CaF2 content 10, 15, 20, and 25 wt%. The rendered page confirms each row and column assignment. Stored numeric cells were checked against the MinerU CSV draft and the render (path b): column 4 rows 2–5 are 0.80, 0.64, 0.53, and 0.41; column 6 rows 2–5 are 10.53, 10.52, 10.50, and 10.53. The QA pre-screen found 16 numeric mismatches among 24 cells against pdftotext; these cells were resolved against the render. The other four numeric columns were not stored because they report phosphorus/sulfur distributions or phosphate capacity, outside this manganese extraction.

The table describes modified BOF slags, not the Na2O addition series. The Na2O series is Figure 6 and is therefore retained as a figure-read candidate without digitization. The text says the Na2O range is 0.15–2.6 wt% and the distribution decreases from 0.42 to 0.32 (PDF page 2, journal page 1117). A later discussion states 2.6 and 0.95 wt% Na2O correspond to 0.37 and 0.32, respectively (PDF page 4, journal page 1119). Those claims do not consistently assign the endpoints, so the latter is retained as typed-refused prose context and no paired Figure 6 points are inferred.

## Conditions and derived quantities

Table 1 states 1300 °C, CO atmosphere, and carbon saturation. It does not print a pressure for these distribution runs or final row-specific slag/metal compositions. The paper's separate 1 atm CO statement is in its activity-coefficient experiment description and was not carried onto Table 1. Figure 6 itself annotates the Na2O series with P_CO = 1 atm and lists MnO ≈1.5–3.5 wt%, CaF2 ≈30 wt%, and SiO2 ≈11.5 wt%; Section II.1 gives carbon-saturated iron with 5.5–6.5 wt% Mn. These series-level statements are retained on the typed-refused figure context, without inventing a CaO balance or per-run metal assays. Numeric final compositions, Na2O activity, and MnO activity coefficient for the Na2O-bearing series are absent from tables; plotted values remain deferred. Figure 7/8 (temperature-dependent distribution/capacity), Figures 9–10 (MnO activity coefficient), and Figure 11 (cross-system comparison including Na2O slags) are useful figure-read candidates.

The distribution values are the paper's reported equilibrium measurements (`measured_direct`). The source does not print the row-level assay pairs or a Table 1 formula for `L_Mn`; the paper labels it the equilibrium manganese distribution. Equation (8) defines the manganous capacity, with `a_Mn(l)` referenced to liquid manganese. Since no existing schema token represents manganous capacity, Table 1's four `log C_Mn2+` values are typed-refused context with the printed relation and reference state. The Na2O text/figure does not resolve complete per-run inputs, so those statements are not admitted as observations. No unprinted pressure, fO2, composition, or metal assay was supplied.
