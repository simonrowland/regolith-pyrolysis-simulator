# Kelley (1953) overlap check

Schumm's phase tables explicitly cite Kelley, Todd, Orr, King, and Bonnickson (1953), report of investigations 4955 (`53KEL/TOD`), for the nepheline S298 and H−H298 values, and for S298 on jadeite, kaliophilite, and leucite. Those entries overlap the cited Kelley compilation. They remain part of Schumm's `compilation_assessed` series; this extract does not reclassify them as Schumm's measurements or duplicate them as new `measured_direct` observations.

The low- and high-albite tables cite later assessed references for their S298, H−H298/S−S298, and ΔfH298 annotations. Microcline and sanidine cite Opeil/Robie/Hemingway references. Their phase audits record these printed attributions; no `53KEL/TOD` annotation appears on those table pages.
