## Typed refusals and schema checks

Table 1(I) reports end-of-run compositions for reactive Fe2O3 runs but never says these rows attained equilibrium. The exact composition token `equilibrium_phase_composition` would overstate what the source says. The table cells remain measured-direct, typed-refused concentration observations with their printed values. This gap is checked against `tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES`, and `simulator/battery/migrate.py::TYPE_QUANTITY`. The same exact-token gap applies to Table 1(II) initial P and final O, and the printed carbon concentration / calculated carbon values. The paper explicitly states slag-metal phosphorus equilibrium for the Na2CO3 runs at the end of the run; only final P and final slag component assays are stored as `equilibrium_phase_composition`. The values use phase tokens `l`, basis `wt_percent`, and canonical unit `%`; this phase-composition quantity retains its printed wt% numeric scale in migration. Five separate E-I experiment records carry each Table 1(II) final slag vector as printed, preserving elemental `T.Fe` without converting it to FeO. The existing phase-row path binds that composition to the point condition and identity for the equilibrium rows. Table 2's E-I results are linked to those runs because the text introduces its calculation and experiment comparison as part of the same Na2CO3 experiment; their composition provenance therefore remains the corresponding page 2 / Table 1(II) row.

## Prose and experimental conditions

The methods report about 250 g Fe-0.1%P alloy, 30 g flux, recrystallized MgO crucible (40 mm ID, 60 mm high), a Tammann furnace and alumina reaction tube, and argon dried over P2O5 and deoxidized through Mg chips heated at 500 °C. Temperatures 1550, 1600, and 1650 °C were measured with Pt-6%Rh/Pt-30%Rh thermocouples. P, O, C, and slag analysis methods are recorded in the extract. The paper prints neither total pressure nor an absolute or buffer-relative oxygen potential nor a gas-mixture ratio; the observations keep those identity conditions unknown. Argon is recorded as the stated atmosphere only.

Fluxes A, B, and C use Na2CO3:SiO2 molar ratios 1:2, 2:3, and 1:1; D is ortho-sodium silicate with Na2O/SiO2 = 1.6 by weight. Fluxes A-D contain Fe2O3 additions up to 40 wt%; detailed mixture values appear in Figure 1 and were not digitized. Fluxes E-I use reagent Na2CO3 plus synthesized Na2O·SiO2; the text gives E as 0.1 mole ratio / 8.8 wt% Na2CO3 and I as 0.5 / 46.5 wt%. The E-I tests are 1600 °C, 15 min.

The four printed author-calculated Na2O-loss intervals are A 7-12%, B 8-17%, C 12-30%, and D 27-44%. They are mass-loss fractions reduced by the text's formula, not time-resolved Na vapor mass loss. The source also states free Na2O evaporated as Na gas until Na2O/SiO2 approached 1 and that deposits contained Na2CO3 plus metallic Na by XRD. It gives no Na mass-versus-time series or sodium vapor pressure.

The explicit prose C-series endpoints, 31 to 20% Na2O as total Fe increased from 9 to 31%, are typed-refused with their paired total-Fe values; no other Figure 3 readings are added. The prose repeats Table 1(I)'s C-2 initial/final P values (0.28 to 0.015%, 0.12 to 0.010%, and 0.05 to 0.007%); these are not duplicated as new observations. The text says final P for E-I was below 0.02%, while Table 1(II) prints 0.020% for E; the cell value is preserved as printed and the textual summary is noted as rounded/inconsistent. The carbon section records initial C <0.003%, the stated measured 0.035-0.038% range, and the authors' calculated 0.085 g / 0.034% increment for Flux E over 6 min, all with the distinctions and typed refusals shown in the extract.

Equations (6)-(8) are preserved as context. Equation (8) is the printed 1600 °C fit `log kP = 8.67 log (%Na2O) - 14.55`; its Figure 11 input points were not digitized, so no derived constant observations are created. The source has no numeric Na2O activity or phosphate-capacity point.
