# Forsberg, Wikström and Rosén (2002) extraction audit

## Scope and disposition

The paper prints two EMF regression equations, two oxide-reaction ΔG° fits, two elemental ΔfG° fits, and one third-law reaction ΔH°298 result. The plotted individual EMF readings in Figure 3 are figure-only and were not digitized. The source's authors own fits/reductions are `measured_reduced`; the fitted EMF inputs are declared `not_printed` at Figure 3. Equation (14) is reaction [11], `2CaO + Fe2O3 → Ca2Fe2O5`, obtained by adding equation (13) to `−2FE` for cell [3]; it is not the cell [3] reaction [10]. The reported reaction ΔH°298 is −17.5 ± 0.4 kJ mol−1, with the paper stating the interval is two standard deviations and that the true uncertainty is likely several kJ/mol. Since this is from component oxides rather than elements, it remains typed-refused under the exact-quantity rule.

The six Table I rows (Gibbs energy functions for CaO, Fe2O3, their improved functions, CaFe2O4 calorimetry and the optimized CaFe2O4 function) and all four Table II rows of CaFe2O4 enthalpy/entropy increments are preserved as auxiliary context. The quoted inputs cite JANAF [9], King [14], Bonnickson [15], Richet and Fiqet [18], Grønvold and Westrum [19], Coughlin et al. [20], and Grønvold and Samuelsen [21]. Own-work Table I/II recalculations remain distinguished from those attributed inputs. The measured reaction entropy uncertainty is ±1.0 J mol−1 K−1, derived from the stated EMF-slope uncertainty; the source gives no nominal entropy-change value in that sentence.

## Schema and migration
