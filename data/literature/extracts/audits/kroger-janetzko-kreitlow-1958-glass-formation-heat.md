## Conditions, reaction and evidence

The article defines the measured process as `Gemenge = Glas + Gas` and describes the heat as referenced to 20°C. Each observation carries `T_C: 20` and a located `temperature_C` point condition. The analyzed glass composition is attached from its printed row where available. No total pressure is printed for these experiments; none is added. For glass observations, the source gives no quench/preservation statement, so `quench_state: not_stated` is recorded with the measurement page. No measurement is presented as in-situ liquid glass.

The direct calorimetry means are `measured_direct`. The two carbon-reaction corrections are `measured_reduced` and link to the corresponding oxidising measurement. Component and glass dissolution heats are measured inputs. The solution-calorimetry results are the authors' own tabulated measurements (`measured_tabulated`); their reducing-condition values are `measured_reduced` from their oxidising solution rows. Their lineage lists stored inputs only. In particular, the older paper is not used as a `derived_from` parent.

The 1956 direct 1:1:6 batch result is 117.8 ± 2.5 cal/g (11780 kcal/100 kg), for the distinct printed glass composition 12.97:11.73:75.30 mass percent Na2O:CaO:SiO2. It is not duplicated here. Reference [2] is Kröger and Kreitlow, Glastechn. Ber. 29 (1956), 393–400, cited for prior quartz, soda and limestone solution heats; those auxiliary values are not repeated as measured glass-formation rows. Reference [3] is Kröger and Janetzko, Z. anorg. allg. Chem. 284 (1956), 83–96. The old calculated “aus Bildungswärmen” comparison is likewise not admitted as a measurement.

## Schema decision

`tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES` admits `gibbs_table`, so the measured rows use that observation type. `simulator/battery/migrate.py::TYPE_QUANTITY` assigns `gibbs_table` a default `delta_fG`; the exact-key set `QUANTITY_ALIASES` (`simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES`) has no token for batch-to-glass-plus-gas reaction heat. Therefore formation rows declare the descriptive non-token `heat_of_glass_formation_from_batch`, which the migrator refuses as quantity-unknown rather than misclassifying as `delta_fG`. `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.DELTA_FH` is not a valid substitute: it means formation from the elements, while the printed reaction begins from a technical batch. The separate measured dissolution inputs use `heat_of_solution_as_printed`, also a non-token; no exact solution-heat quantity exists in the migration aliases. `Quantity.ENTHALPY_OF_FUSION` (`simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.ENTHALPY_OF_FUSION`) is not a substitute because these are dissolution heats, not fusion heats. No near-miss quantity token is assigned.

There is no phase gap: `simulator/battery/migrate.py::PHASE_MAP` recognizes `glass`, and `simulator/battery/enums.py::PhaseRole.GLASS` is an existing role. Each glass is stored as the measured glass phase. Tabelle 12a's glass composition entries are printed in kilograms and remain plain printed maps, without a percent or mole-fraction basis; they are not converted to a different quantity or used to claim a phase-composition observation.

The isolated migration yields 35 observations, 0 hard issues, 35 unavailable typed refusals and exactly 35 quantity-unknown observations; no phase is unknown. Every observation retains its 20°C point condition with the section 8 locator; printed glass compositions and the not-stated quench state reach each applicable observation. Pressure remains absent. The expected battery-store delta is 35 refused quantity-unknown observations and 0 admitted scored observations. No store regeneration was committed.
