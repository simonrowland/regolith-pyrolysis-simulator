# Chen & Presnall (1975) table and landmark audit

Tables 1, 2, and 3 were transcribed from rendered PDF pages 3, 4, and 6 (printed pages 400, 401, and 403) and checked cell by cell against the 200, 250, and 300 dpi crops, respectively. The in-repository cell-audit CSV contains all 71 table run rows plus the eight prose landmarks, both printed binary composition components, row run conditions, and phases. Numeric tokens are stored as strings so their source spellings remain exact and were checked using Decimal. Tables 1–3 do not print run numbers; audit row indexes are internal labels only. Table 1's ** mark means identified by X-ray powder diffraction; Table 3's separate ** mark means the charge was quenched and transferred to a new platinum capsule. Table 2's footnote says quartz is universally present.

The eight prose landmarks remain in the extract as non-scored phase_equilibrium_run_products context; six use model_derived or author_estimate classes, and the two one-atmosphere temperatures remain measured_tabulated. i1 and both one-atmosphere locators were checked against their corrected printed-page crops. The 27 kbar extrapolation now carries composition A (56.44 wt% SiO2, 43.56 wt% MgO) and the extrapolation flag; published page 404 repeats the composition rounded to 56.4 wt% SiO2 and 43.6 wt% MgO. Pressure in Pa is an inferred conversion: kbar × 100,000,000 Pa/kbar; 1 atm × 101,325 Pa/atm. No plotted figure values were digitized. The phase-boundary placements remain author estimates or extrapolations from the prose.
