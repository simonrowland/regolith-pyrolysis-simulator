## Source statements, equations and fusion data

The reference state is `H(T) − H(298.16)`; the paper reports defined calories per mole and states `1 cal. = 4.1840 abs. joules`. No experiment total pressure is printed and none is added. The measured heat-content values are stored in their printed cal/mol; the migrator's canonical values are in kJ per declared mole basis.

CaFe2O4 melts incongruently. The text reports melting evidence as low as 1,463 K, incomplete melting at 1,530 K, and an arbitrary intermediate adopted melting temperature of 1,510 K. It gives heat of fusion 25,870 cal/mol and entropy of fusion 17.13 cal/(deg mol). The equations are printed as:

- `CaFe2O4(c)`: `H_T − H_298.16 = 39.42T + 2.38 × 10^-3 T^2 + 3.66 × 10^-5 T^-1 − 13,194`; (0.3%; 298–1510 K).
- `CaFe2O4(l)`: `H_T − H_298.16 = 54.90T − 5030`; (0.1%; 1510–1800 K).

Ca2Fe2O5 also melts incongruently. The text reports no evidence of melting below 1,704 K, complete melting at 1,752 K, and an adopted melting point of 1,750 K. It gives heat of fusion 36,110 cal/mol and entropy of fusion 20.63 cal/(deg mol). The equations are printed as:

- `Ca2Fe2O5(c)`: `H_T − H_298.16 = 59.24T + 11.68 × 10^5 T^-1 − 21,580`; (0.5%; 298–1750 K).
- `CaFe2O5(l)` (printed): `H_T − H_293.16 = 74.20T − 10,980`; (0.1%; 1750–1850 K). The printed H_293.16 is a probable misprint for H_298.16 and is preserved verbatim in the extract.

The preceding paragraph ties that liquid fit to Ca2Fe2O5. Its printed text says, “In the present work there was no evidence of melting below 1704°K., and melting was complete at 1752°K.” This matches the fit's 1750–1850 K interval. Per controller ruling, the observation is associated with Ca2Fe2O5 while preserving `CaFe2O5(l)` verbatim in `phase_as_printed` and noting the apparent formula misprint. The paper studies only CaFe2O4, Ca2Fe2O5 and MgFe2O4; the printed CaFe2O5 formula is not charge-balanced under the stated ferric oxide materials (Ca2+ + 2Fe3+ = +8 versus O5 = −10).

Table II values are the paper's smooth heat-content values and are stored as measured-reduced rows, with lineage to the corresponding Table I observation groups used in the fit. Table I values are the authors' own experimental measurements (measured-direct). Table II's entropies and the article's heat-capacity discussion are outside the requested heat-content quantity; the two printed entropy-of-fusion values are preserved above as context. The plotted curves in Fig. 1 are figure-only candidates and were not digitized. No other same-compound numeric values are attributed to other literature in this article; Table I and Table II are the in-paper experimental/smoothed records, and their listed temperatures do not coincide for direct same-temperature value pairs.
