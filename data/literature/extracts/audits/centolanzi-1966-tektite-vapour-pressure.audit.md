## Evidence class and schema decisions

The Figure 7 entries are authors' printed steady-state slopes from time traces whose numeric plotted points are not transcribed, so they are `author_derived`, not `measured_reduced` with missing parent rows. The exact marker-to-value list and legend unit are separately recorded from the 600 dpi Figure 7 render, and the observation rows preserve the prose spelling too. Ag, Au, silica, and tektite glass identify the compared sample materials only; the vapor species are not printed, so all rows migrate with unknown vapor species. The conditional `12` rate is explicitly conditional on Walter's bubble-pressure function and remains a separate `author_derived` typed refusal. The tektite fit and the Figure 13(b) bound are also the authors' outputs; no pressure values were evaluated from the fit and no model reduction was performed in this extraction. The prior Chapman–Larson equation and Hidalgo silica relation are attributed quotes.

There is no exact area-normalized mass-loss-rate token. `rate_series` is an allowed observation type, but `Quantity.MASS_LOSS_RATE` is mass/time, `Quantity.MASS_LOSS_AREAL_DENSITY` is mass/area, and `_convert_rate_to_kg_per_s` has no area/time unit conversion. The extract retains the printed numeric strings and units under the descriptive quantity `areal_mass_loss_rate_as_printed`, with explicit typed refusals. This is checked against `tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::TYPE_QUANTITY`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.MASS_LOSS_RATE`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.MASS_LOSS_AREAL_DENSITY`, and `simulator/battery/migrate.py::_convert_rate_to_kg_per_s`; the existing areal-flux schema-gap precedent is `data/literature/extracts/audits/kems-010-richter-2007.mapping.yaml`.

The paper names only total vapor pressure for a multicomponent tektite vapor and supplies no vapor molecule. It is not relabeled as Na, K, SiO, or SiO2 gas. The exact p_sat candidate is retained with `gas_basis: TOTAL_PRESSURE_not_species`, which the current migrator refuses in `simulator/battery/migrate.py::_quantity_contradiction`. The same reason applies to the attributed total-pressure fits. The silica pressure equation remains attributed context because this paper states no gas species or source-specific phase for Hidalgo's equation.

Temperature conditions are stored as `point_conditions.temperature_K` with a state and locator. Explicit furnace and stagnation pressures are retained as printed in the audit and converted to `point_conditions.total_pressure_Pa` by the exact factor 101325 Pa/atm, with no inferred pressure added. Separate experiment records keep the Figure 7 furnace conditions and Figure 13(b) test conditions attached to their observations; the aggregate and attributed fits have separate unknown condition records so neither inherits a neighboring run's pressure. Figure 13(c)'s `P_s` is stored as the explicitly printed stagnation condition; its unreported local shoulder pressure is left absent. The fit's 1700–3000°K interval is a validity range, not a point temperature. Composition is qualitative (“mainly silica”), so no mole-fraction identity is claimed. No fO2 or standard state is stated.
