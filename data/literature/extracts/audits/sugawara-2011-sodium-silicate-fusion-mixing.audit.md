## Methods, evidence, conditions, and reference states

- The Table 1 fusion results are the authors' corrected DSC measurements. The paper states DSC-320, a platinum cell, a 2 K/min heating rate, calibration against Al, Ag, and Au fusion enthalpies, and a premelting correction based on Berman and Brown [40] and drop-calorimetry enthalpies [37, 38]. It does not print the raw apparent peak areas or numeric correction. Under the rule for author-calculated results whose raw inputs cannot be stored, these corrected outputs are `quoted_attributed`; the attribution says they are the authors' results and explains the missing raw inputs.
- The Table 1 liquid mixing values are calculated from the measured fusion values and Table 2 thermodynamic inputs using equations (2) and (16). They are `measured_reduced`, with their stored fusion and Table 2 inputs in the derivation. Their exact printed quantity is integral liquid enthalpy of mixing relative to pure liquid Na2O and SiO2 endmembers, as defined by equation (2). The paper states the mixing enthalpy is temperature-independent under its no-heat-capacity-of-mixing assumption; no single temperature is assigned to these three values.
- The six prose fusion values and the weighted mean preserve the paper's stated methods and attributions. The cited comparison values remain `quoted_attributed`. The prose does not give a temperature, batch composition, total pressure, or fO2 for these values, so those conditions remain unknown.
- Table 2 liquid Cp values are attributed to [38] for the first four compositions and [47] for Na2O. Figure 1 identifies the [38] measurements as drop calorimetry; the text describes those Cp values as temperature-independent. The four solid Cp functions are attributed to [49]; this article does not state that reference's method or a validity interval. Their temperature condition therefore remains unknown rather than assigning an assumed range.
- The two Table 2 endmember fusion enthalpies are quoted from [50] and [47]; the article does not state those sources' methods. The three intermediate Table 2 fusion enthalpies are `model_derived` from the fitted liquid mixing enthalpy and endmember data, as Table 2 footnote f states. Their derivation names the stored input observations, including the matching mixture and endmember solid-enthalpy rows.
- Table 3 stores ten fitted model coefficients, not measured rows: h2 −271.2 (117.2), h3 3991.5 (367.2), h4 −6177.3 (550.9), h5 3920.7 (398.7), h6 −932.8 (112.0) kJ/mol; s2 −1947.0 (161.6), s3 5972.7 (485.4), s4 −8320.5 (707.1), s5 5468.3 (500.4), s6 −1392.9 (138.1) J/K mol. The paper states a least-squares weighted fit to measured enthalpy, Na2O activity, liquidus, and immiscibility data. The coefficients define the fifth-order enthalpy and entropy polynomials in equations (8) and (9); equation (14) gives partial molar Gibbs energy and activity. Parenthetical values are the printed probability errors; the confidence level is not stated. Figure-only fit inputs are not digitized.
- All observation `point_conditions` carry located temperature, composition, fO2, and pressure states. Composition is bound on Table 1 and Table 2 rows where printed. Temperatures are unknown for prose values without a stated value, the Cp equations without a range, and the liquid Cp values with no single T. Total pressure and fO2 remain unknown throughout; none is assumed. No glass or quench observation is reported in this extraction.
