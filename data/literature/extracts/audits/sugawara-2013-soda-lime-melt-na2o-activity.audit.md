# Sugawara et al. (2013) extraction audit

Source: Sugawara et al., “Na2O activity and thermodynamic mixing properties of SiO2–Na2O–CaO melt,” *Journal of Non-Crystalline Solids* 371–372 (2013), 58–65. Printed table locators below use the absolute PDF page number and the caption's table number.

## Machine reads and cell audit

Transcription started with the MinerU table HTML/markdown and was cross-read against `pdftotext-layout.txt`. The MinerU draft CSVs in `tables/sugawara-2013-soda-lime-melt-na2o-activity/` preserve the machine-readable table cells. `sugawara-2013-soda-lime-melt-na2o-activity.cells.csv` records 876 audited draft cells: 843 agree between MinerU and pdftotext on digits, sign, and precision, with each table's row/column layout checked once against its render; 33 cells were resolved directly against a PDF render. The table QA pre-screen reported numeric digit agreement 1.000 and no flagged cells for each table (1–4).

The compare tool's output is committed in `sugawara-2013-soda-lime-melt-na2o-activity.compare.txt`. On the canonical extract it reports 38 mismatches because `tools/mineru_table_draft.py::_phase_matches_column` does not recognize the migrator's canonical phase token `l` (it accepts `gas`, `liquid`, and `solid`). A scratch-only comparison with that token changed to `liquid` matched 23 numeric activity/enthalpy cells and left 15 entropy fits as `needs_mapping`; there were no remaining mismatched values, locator problems, or unassigned candidates. Those 15 are the intentional schema-refused partial-molar entropy fits described below. The committed extract keeps canonical `l`.

## Extracted content and evidence decisions

- Table 1's 17 sample/reference rows retain every printed nominal and EDX composition cell, printed `mol%` units, sample/run labels, and blank states. The paper reports EDX on quenched glass; phase is `glass`, quench by water is recorded from page 2, and preservation is `not_stated`. Composition cells stay as printed table context rather than being divided by 100. The exact phase-basis token `mole_percent` is absent from `simulator/battery/enums.py::PHASE_BASIS_TOKENS`, although `AmountBasis.MOLE_PERCENT` exists; `simulator/battery/migrate.py::_phase_row_rejection` rejects that basis for a phase-composition observation. The existing `equilibrium_phase_composition` route in `tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.EQUILIBRIUM_PHASE_COMPOSITION`, and `simulator/battery/enums.py::AmountBasis.MOLE_FRACTION` does not preserve the printed mole-percent basis without a conversion, so the exact measured cells remain in context. The existing composition extract `data/literature/extracts/park-2021-vanadium-thermodynamics-thesis.yaml` stores this type of quantity as mole fractions, not the printed mole-percent values used here.
- Table 2's 24 paired-cell rows retain the sample/reference nominal compositions, all 82 printed EMF values with units and temperatures, and 62 blank EMF cells. The EMF is measured directly in the high-temperature melt (`l`). There is no exact migrator observation type/quantity combination for the printed EMF series: see `tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES`, and `simulator/battery/migrate.py::TYPE_QUANTITY`. The full numeric table therefore remains typed table context.
- Table 3 stores all eight printed log10 activities, both absent cells, each row's sample and reference melt composition, and the printed reference to the activity of 70SiO2–30Na2O from compilation [27] used by Eq. (3). The paper gives no thermodynamic standard state; it remains unknown. Five values are the authors' outputs from their EMF calculation, but the needed EMF inputs have no typed observation route, so they are `quoted_attributed`; the three values attributed to [27] are also `quoted_attributed`. This avoids calling an output measured-reduced without stored measurement observations.
- Table 4 retains 20 printed rows and six temperature columns per row: 77 numeric RT ln cells, 42 absent cells, and one uncertainty-only `±4.4` cell, for 120 expanded points. It also retains all 15 partial-molar enthalpy and 15 partial-molar entropy fit cells and their uncertainties. Twelve fit pairs are `model_derived` from the exact Table 4 RT ln parent observations named in `derived_from`; the three fits attributed to [27] are `quoted_attributed`. Fit temperature columns and Eq. (6) are preserved. Printed uncertainty widths are stored as printed, with confidence level recorded as not stated.
- The printed partial-molar Gibbs energy and partial-molar entropy do not have exact quantity tokens. `Quantity.DELTA_FG` is not the printed partial-molar Gibbs energy, and `Quantity.S` is not the printed partial-molar entropy; see `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.DELTA_FG`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.S`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES`, and `simulator/battery/migrate.py::TYPE_QUANTITY`. The Table 4 RT ln points and entropy-fit rows therefore use descriptive non-token quantity labels and remain typed refusals; the test asserts that the unknown-quantity observation set is exactly these 120 RT ln points plus 15 entropy fits. The 15 enthalpy fits use `Quantity.PARTIAL_MOLAR_ENTHALPY`.
- Equations (1)–(14), their printed expressions and locators, and the Table 4 Eq. (6) fit relation are retained. Eq. (3)'s printed final-term sign is preserved, with the paper's internal algebraic inconsistency noted rather than corrected. The page 7 quoted and calculated Fe voltammetry peak-potential differences are retained as context; no exact electrochemical observation type/quantity exists (`tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity`), so they are not migrated as scored observations.
- Figs. 2–9 are listed as `figure_only`, with their captions/axes and plot-only status; no plotted series was digitized. The prose log-activity statement and redox-potential context are preserved with their page/section locators.

Only conditions stated by the paper are attached. The EMF experiment's printed dry-air atmosphere and its reported flow are retained; the flow is not treated as a gas ratio or pressure. The paper gives no total pressure, oxygen partial pressure/fugacity, or activity standard state, so those axes remain unknown. Table 1 glass rows record the water quench and page with preservation status `not_stated`; in-situ high-temperature rows use phase `l`.

## Migration and verification

The real isolated migrator produced 158 observations: 23 numeric (`POINT`) and 135 unavailable, with 0 hard issues and 0 registry issues. The 23 numeric values are eight Table 3 activities and 15 Table 4 enthalpy fits. The 135 unavailable values are the 120 schema-refused Table 4 RT ln points and 15 schema-refused entropy fits. There are 84 soft `IDENTITY_INCOMPLETE` issues because the source omits required standard-state and/or pressure/fO2 inputs; no missing condition was inferred. Three Table 3 activity identities were checked after migration: T=1473/1573 K, printed sample composition 80SiO2–20Na2O normalized by the existing migrator to SiO2 0.8 / Na2O 0.2, and unknown total pressure, fO2, and standard state.
