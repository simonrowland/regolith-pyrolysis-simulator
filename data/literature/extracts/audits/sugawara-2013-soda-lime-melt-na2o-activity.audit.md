## Extracted content and evidence decisions

- Table 1's 17 sample/reference rows retain every printed nominal and EDX composition cell, printed `mol%` units, sample/run labels, and blank states. The paper reports EDX on quenched glass; phase is `glass`, quench by water is recorded from page 2, and preservation is `not_stated`. Composition cells stay as printed table context rather than being divided by 100. The exact phase-basis token `mole_percent` is absent from `simulator/battery/enums.py::PHASE_BASIS_TOKENS`, although `AmountBasis.MOLE_PERCENT` exists; `simulator/battery/migrate.py::_phase_row_rejection` rejects that basis for a phase-composition observation. The existing `equilibrium_phase_composition` route in `tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.EQUILIBRIUM_PHASE_COMPOSITION`, and `simulator/battery/enums.py::AmountBasis.MOLE_FRACTION` does not preserve the printed mole-percent basis without a conversion, so the exact measured cells remain in context. The existing composition extract `data/literature/extracts/park-2021-vanadium-thermodynamics-thesis.yaml` stores this type of quantity as mole fractions, not the printed mole-percent values used here.
- Table 2's 24 paired-cell rows retain the sample/reference nominal compositions, all 82 printed EMF values with units and temperatures, and 62 blank EMF cells. The reference composition follows the printed SiO2 / Na2O / Al2O3 header for every run. The EMF is measured directly in the high-temperature melt (`l`). There is no exact migrator observation type/quantity combination for the printed EMF series: see `tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES`, and `simulator/battery/migrate.py::TYPE_QUANTITY`. The full numeric table therefore remains typed table context.
- Table 3 stores all eight printed log10 activities, both absent cells, each row's sample and reference melt composition, and the printed reference to the activity of 70SiO2–30Na2O from compilation [27] used by Eq. (3). The paper does not name a standard state in words; the reference state is inferred from print: 2013 Eq. (3) anchors every activity to the 70SiO2–30Na2O melt using a(Na2O) from compilation [27], and Eq. (4) defines RT ln a(Na2O) as the partial molar Gibbs energy of mixing; [27] (Sugawara et al. 2011) Eqs. (2)–(3) and (14) formulate that mixing for liquid Na2O–SiO2, so the reference is pure liquid Na2O (see the reference-state paragraphs below). Five values are the authors' outputs from their EMF calculation, but the needed EMF inputs have no typed observation route, so they are `quoted_attributed`; the three values attributed to [27] are also `quoted_attributed`. The three [27] rows retain their printed temperatures and sample compositions; their atmosphere, total pressure, and fO2 remain unknown at the Table 3 locator. Dry air is attached only to this study's EMF-derived activity rows. This avoids calling an output measured-reduced without stored measurement observations.
- The three Table 3 [27] activity widths and the ten Table 4 enthalpy and ten entropy fit widths are copied into the observation-level uncertainty input as printed, with confidence level recorded as not stated. The entropy-fit values remain unavailable, but their uncertainty records are preserved through migration.
- Table 4 retains 20 printed rows and six temperature columns per row: 77 numeric RT ln cells, 42 absent cells, and one uncertainty-only `±4.4` cell, for 120 expanded points. It also retains all 15 partial-molar enthalpy and 15 partial-molar entropy fit cells and their uncertainties. Twelve fit pairs are `model_derived` from the exact Table 4 RT ln parent observations named in `derived_from`; the three fits attributed to [27] are `quoted_attributed`. Fit temperature columns and Eq. (6) are preserved. Printed uncertainty widths are stored as printed, with confidence level recorded as not stated.
- The printed partial-molar Gibbs energy and partial-molar entropy do not have exact quantity tokens. `Quantity.DELTA_FG` is not the printed partial-molar Gibbs energy, and `Quantity.S` is not the printed partial-molar entropy; see `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.DELTA_FG`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity.S`, `simulator/battery/migrate.py::QUANTITY_ALIASES`, and `simulator/battery/migrate.py::TYPE_QUANTITY`. The Table 4 RT ln points and entropy-fit rows therefore use descriptive non-token quantity labels and remain typed refusals; the test asserts that the unknown-quantity observation set is exactly these 120 RT ln points plus 15 entropy fits. The 15 enthalpy fits use `Quantity.PARTIAL_MOLAR_ENTHALPY`.
- Equations (1)–(14), their printed expressions and locators, and the Table 4 Eq. (6) fit relation are retained. Eq. (3)'s printed final-term sign is preserved, with the paper's internal algebraic inconsistency noted rather than corrected. The page 7 quoted and calculated Fe voltammetry peak-potential differences are retained as context; no exact electrochemical observation type/quantity exists (`tools/validate_literature_extracts.py::OBSERVATION_TYPES`, `simulator/battery/enums.py::Quantity`), so they are not migrated as scored observations.
- Figs. 2–9 are listed as `figure_only`, with their captions/axes and plot-only status; no plotted series was digitized. The prose log-activity statement and redox-potential context are preserved with their page/section locators.

Only conditions stated by the paper are attached. The EMF experiment's printed dry-air atmosphere and its reported flow are retained for this study's own EMF-derived activities; the flow is not treated as a gas ratio or pressure. Table 3 quotes three activities from [27], and this paper does not state the source experiment's atmosphere, total pressure, or fO2; those fields are typed unknown at the Table 3 locator. The standard state for all eight Table 3 activities is now typed as Raoultian pure-endmember liquid Na2O at each row's temperature, inferred from Sugawara et al. (2013), Eqs. (3)–(4), and [27], Section 3, Eq. (14). Provenance on the five “This study” values records that the 70SiO2–30Na2O activity is evaluated from [27]'s fitted Gibbs-energy-of-mixing model, not directly measured. Total pressure and fO2 remain unknown where unreported. Table 1 glass rows record the water quench and page with preservation status `not_stated`; in-situ high-temperature rows use phase `l`.
