## Duplicate verdict

Not a duplicate. PDF page 3 (published page 72) is Shornikov and Slobodov, Thermodynamic properties of the K2O–Al2O3 melts, Experiment in Geosciences 28(1), 72–75. The adjacent Na2O–Al2O3 article in the same issue is a different paper (different authors, pages 69–71) and is extracted separately. pdfinfo Title is the journal file name `titul2c_no_kaz2022.cdr`, so the verdict uses the printed title and page, not the file title.

## Context only

- Roth and Moya "above 2500 K" is a bound the paper rejects. Not a point.
- Mixing-energy minima −95 kJ/mol at 1450 K and −65 kJ/mol at 2300 K, near KAlO2. No mixing-energy quantity. Composition is not a mole fraction. Parents are not printed.
- p(KAlO) does not exceed 3.5×10^−9 atm at 2300 K. A ceiling over the melts generally, not a point at a composition.
- Activity reference: the paper does not state pure solid versus pure liquid. Expected migration phase: unknown. No activity row was admitted.
