# Shornikov, Yakovlev and Minaev (2022), Na2O–Al2O3: extraction audit

## Duplicate verdict

Not a duplicate of the K2O–Al2O3 article in the same issue. PDF page 3 is published page 69: Shornikov, Yakovlev and Minaev, Thermodynamic properties of the Na2O–Al2O3 melts. The K2O article begins on published page 72 and is a different source id. The Na2O PDF continues into the first page of the K2O article; those K2O numbers are not copied here.

## Admitted

Three quoted NaAlO2 melting temperatures, all on published page 69. The paper does not choose among them.

| id | value | attribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| na2o_2022_naalO2_tm_matignon | 1923±20 K | Matignon (1923) |
| na2o_2022_naalO2_tm_schairer | 2123±30 K | Schairer and Bowen (1956) |
| na2o_2022_naalO2_tm_weber | 2140±6 K | Weber and Venero (1970) |

The 600 dpi render of PDF page 3 shows the same three numbers.

## Context only

- Mixing-energy minima −77 kJ/mol at 1450 K near NaAlO2, and −60 kJ/mol at 2300 K. No mixing-energy quantity. Parents not printed.
- The 10^3 factor between p(Na) over Na2O and over NaAlO2 is a comparison of curves, not a pressure.
- Activity reference phase is not stated. Expected migration phase: unknown. No activity row was admitted.
