# Shornikov and Yakovlev 2025 CAI evaporation — extraction audit

Source: Shornikov, S., and O. Yakovlev (2025), Mass spectrometric investigation of evaporation of the Ca–Al–inclusions of chondrite melts, Springer chapter, doi 10.1007/978-3-031-87558-8_24, published pages 119–122. Six PDF pages. Pages 1–2 are blank and the book copyright page. This is not Geochemistry International 63(9):810–836 (shornikov-2025-ca-al-inclusion-thermodynamics) and not the adjacent alkali chapter doi 10.1007/978-3-031-87558-8_23 (shornikov-2025-saratov-alkali-evaporation).

The 2023 Efremovka abstracts (shornikov-2023-cai-melt-evaporation and shornikov-2023-natural-cai-evaporation) are shorter writeups of the same experiment. Their figure digitisation was deferred and they do not carry these curves as numbers. This chapter was still read. No number from those abstracts was copied.

## Tables

MinerU produced no table. The ion list on published page 120 is prose.

## Figures

All five figures were rendered at 600 dpi (PDF pages 4–6). None was stored as numbers.

Fig. 1 (published p. 120) is this chapter's partial pressures of Ca, Mg, Al, and SiO over CAI types A and B. The ordinate is already lg p_i in atm, with labeled ticks at −5, −6, and −7. The abscissa is T from 1800 to 2600 K. A trial calibration put 200 K at 429.25 px on panel A (label-center residual under 1 K) and one lg unit at 394 px (−5 at y = 4108, −6 at y = 4502, −7 at y = 4896). Disks are about 20–29 px in radius, so a clean center would be about 0.07 in lg p and about 12 K. The distance transform did not give that read. It returned two peaks on some disks, about 0.15 in lg p apart, and only four blue peaks in panel A where the Al curve continues through several more markers. Those peaks were discarded. No partial pressure is stored.

Fig. 2 is the residual-melt composition in mol %. It is not an activity, pressure, activity coefficient, partial Gibbs energy, or evaporation coefficient. It was not digitized.

Fig. 3 plots lg a_i for CaO, Al2O3, SiO2, and calculated gehlenite. The oxide activities are this chapter's determination. The same marker failure applies, so they were not digitized. Gehlenite, anorthite, and grossular were calculated from Shornikov (2007). The printed comparison, anorthite and grossular at 2500 K lower than gehlenite by 10^5 and 10^11, is kept as that sentence and not as an activity.

Fig. 4 is the Murchison comparison attributed to Yakovlev et al. (1987). It was not digitized.

Fig. 5 is the oxygen pressure. The text calculates atomic oxygen from WO2 + O = WO3. The markers were not read.

## Standard state

The activity sentence on published page 120 is: the CaO, Al2O3, and SiO2 activities were determined (Fig. 3). The chapter does not state a pure solid or pure liquid oxide, and it does not say the reference was measured over the pure oxide at the experiment temperature. The 2026-10-20 pure-oxide-at-T inference was not used. Because no activity number was stored, migration has no activity row to resolve. The prose is on the Fig. 3 context row so the refusal stays with the figure.
