## Reconciliation

- Table 5 has 9 printed condition rows and 27 numeric Na2O-rate cells (45 rate positions, 18 printed dashes); only Na2O component-rate columns are extracted, not the Na2SO4 comparison column or activity footer.
- Table 6 has 12 printed condition/composition rows and 12 numeric K2O-rate cells; B2O3 cells are excluded.
- Composition and temperature are retained per observation. SO3, PH2O and PSO2 conditions are retained verbatim in each row. The source gives no total pressure, flow rate, velocity, geometry, exposed area, or vapor species; those fields are absent.
- Schema gap: the evaporation-flux identity reader accepts `composition_as_printed` as a component amount map, but interprets its numeric entries as `MOL_INVENTORY`; it does not read the point-condition `composition` map or a printed mass-percent composition string as a mass-percent map. The source prints mass-percent Na2O glass composition and mole-percent K2O composition. No unit/basis conversion or identity map was invented. Migrated identity composition and sweep gas therefore remain unknown and cause expected incomplete-input validation; total pressure is also unknown because it is not printed.
- The source discusses rapid gas flow in the method description but does not assign a printed flow rate to these table rows. No flow or geometry is inferred.
- `mineru_table_draft.py compare` output is committed alongside this report. It reports zero compared cells because it only scans numeric `values.points` arrays while this extract uses one scalar observation per printed table cell. Thus the committed comparison is a presence-only tool result; the full cell reconciliation above is the audit evidence.
- Figure-read candidate: Figures 3–5 show additional plotted Na2O rates; no figure coordinates were extracted.

## Isolated migration result

- 39 observations migrated; 39 scalar POINT values; 0 unavailable values.
- 117 validation issues, all `identity_incomplete`: 39 composition identities (the schema gap above), 39 total-pressure identities (not printed), and 39 sweep-gas identities (the printed H2O/SO2 partial pressures do not identify the full atmosphere). No other validation issue class occurred.
- Temperature reached every migrated observation as 1673.15 K (Table 5) or 1273.15 K (Table 6); printed atmosphere and composition conditions also reached each observation through located `point_conditions`.
- Scorer check: all 39 rows refuse with `missing_input` because flow/geometry inputs are absent; the printed Na2O and K2O component labels have no evaporation-flux scoring rail.
- Expected observation-store delta: +39 evaporation-flux observations across 2 experiments; no store regeneration committed.
