# Mendybaev et al. (2017): transcription provenance

## Bibliographic reference

Mendybaev, R. A., Williams, C. D., Spicuzza, M. J., Richter, F. M., Valley,
J. W., Fedkin, A. V. & Wadhwa, M. (2017). Thermal and chemical evolution in
the early Solar System as recorded by FUN CAIs: Part II — Laboratory
evaporation of potential CMS-1 precursor material. *Geochimica et
Cosmochimica Acta* **201**, 49–64. DOI 10.1016/j.gca.2016.08.034
([resolver](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gca.2016.08.034)).

## Source identity and page

The corpus PDF is the submitted/revised manuscript, not the typeset journal
article. It has 59 pages. The physical PDF page used for Tables 1 and 2 is
page 39 (zero-based `pdf_page_index: 38`); this manuscript page has no separate
printed folio. The journal citation's folio range is 49–64. The table-cell
values in the simulator extract were checked against a rendered image of PDF
page 39. Table 1 contains the FUNC residue compositions; Table 2 begins below
it on the same page.
