# Barin 1995 aluminate and spinel tables

Source: Barin, I. (1995). *Thermochemical Data of Pure Substances*, 3rd ed. VCH.

**Source PDF:** `barin-1995.pdf` in the offline regolith corpus. SHA-256:
`36440a97bf06c3406790119f0a82f9de7554a6c6af3b1064ecaa5c5e4b075739`.

## Coverage

The row-by-row transcription of the six aluminate and spinel tables is held in
the corpus at
`tables/barin-1995-aluminates/barin-1995-aluminate-tables.md`. It covers the
printed headings CaAl2O4, CaAl4O7, Ca3Al2O6, Ca12Al14O33, NaAlO2, and MgAl2O4.
These entries have no numbered table captions; the formula headings identify
the tables.

The transcription covers physical PDF pages 557–560 (printed folios 442–445),
physical page 1131 (folio 1015), and physical page 1248 (folio 1132). For the
calcium aluminates, the page sequence includes gaps where the volume continues
with other substances. The source's page numbering and the PDF page index are
both retained in the corpus transcription and in the extract locators.

## Repository data

The evaluated values remain in `data/literature/extracts/barin-1995.yaml`.
Each locator retains its physical page, printed folio, PDF page index, printed
row, and printed column. Temperature conditions retain their own table-row and
column coordinates, so the temperature and measured value identify their
respective cells. Fidelity samples in the extract continue to pin selected
cells, including transition temperatures and fusion increments.

The extract's `source_path` points to the corpus-relative transcription path
above. The full table-by-table transcription is intentionally not duplicated
in this public repository. Resolve the corpus path through the configured
regolith-corpus overlay when source text is needed.
