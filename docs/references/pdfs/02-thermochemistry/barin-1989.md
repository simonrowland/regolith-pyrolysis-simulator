# Barin 1989 — first edition

**Citation:** Barin, I. (1989). *Thermochemical Data of Pure Substances*, 1st ed., Parts I and II. VCH, Weinheim and New York. ISBN 3-527-27812-5 and 0-89573-866-X.

Publisher bibliographic confirmation: Wiley's record for the 1990 review of
the book gives the 1989 VCH publication, both parts, and both ISBNs:
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ange.19901020738. The 1995 Wiley
book record identifies the later work as the third edition:
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9783527619825.

Acquisition note: this is distinct from `barin-1995`, which is the third
edition. No DOI is assigned to the 1989 book. Acquire and identify the 1989
edition separately in the corpus before using it as source evidence; do not
copy or relabel the 1995 corpus files.
