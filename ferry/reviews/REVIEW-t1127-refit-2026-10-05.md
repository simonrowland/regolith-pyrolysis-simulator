### (2) Were the old Stull values a units/conversion error or a different dataset? **A genuinely different (obsolete) dataset. VERIFIED by arithmetic.**
- The conversion was done correctly. The yaml quoted the NIST WebBook form log10(P/bar) = 5.73623 − 13204.109/(T − 24.306) (Al) and 9.56436 − 23308.848/(T − 123.133) (Si), and stored A+5 for Pa (10.73623, 14.56436). That is the right bar→Pa step.

- A °C/K confusion is also ruled out. It would shift T by a constant 273.15 K, but the old 1-atm temperatures plus 273.15 give 2601.6 K (Al) and 2834.8 K (Si), against JANAF 2793.7 and 3508.1 K.
