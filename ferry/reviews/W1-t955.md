## Intent

condensed-phase standard-state properties via the **pure-phase** consumer; it
is not a scoring reference for VapoRock (as run), IMCC, or internal-analytical
via `engine_point`. JANAF rows are single pure substances, so the melt-
composition `engine_point` consumer does not apply. Readiness previously
counted every NIST-JANAF experiment (~1,706) as an `engine_point` GAP.

## Change

Data-driven predicate (no source-id substring):

1. `identity.composition` is `not_applicable`
2. `derivation.relation` stamps `engine_reference_input=true`
3. `derivation.relation` stamps `scoring_eligible=false`

When all three hold, every `engine_point` engine row is
`ReadinessStatus.NOT_APPLICABLE` with
`GapReason.PURE_SUBSTANCE_REFERENCE` and missing text
`pure-substance reference table; scored by the pure-phase consumer, not engine_point`.

Files:

- `simulator/battery/waypoints.py` — predicate + `_consumer_constraints` early N/A
- `scripts/bench_readiness.py` — store-level override from any matching observation
  on the experiment (JANAF series rows have no `point_conditions`)
