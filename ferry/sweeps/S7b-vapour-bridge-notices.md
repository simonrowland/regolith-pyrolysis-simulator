**Scope (directory split):** `simulator/vapour_rail/`, vapour bridge helpers under `simulator/melt_backend/` (`_vapor_pressures_via_vaporock_or_antoine` on alphaMELTS / ThermoEngine), and the VapoRock VAPOR_PRESSURE shadow provider (`engines/vaporock/`, registered from `simulator/core.py`).  
**Out of this file:** whole-repo S7 / battery-runner notice drops → `S7-notice-dropped-in-transit.md` (do not overwrite).  
**Predicate:** an engine/adapter attaches a notice, warning, authority level, domain flag, or refusal reason, and an intermediary (bridge, provider, cache/store, runner, serializer, merge) discards it, so the consumer sees an **unflagged** value.  
**Project doctrine:** predict-and-flag — a flagged value must stay flagged all the way to the ledger.  
**Seed:** VapoRock’s commissioning notice (`authority=extrapolated` + `CommissioningNotice` warning + `certified_band`) is dropped by the vapour bridge and by the shadow provider projection.

**Corpus note:** Attachment site is `simulator/melt_backend/vaporock.py` `equilibrate` (~1830–1845): after domain refusals, `assess_engine_commissioning('vaporock', …)` stamps `diagnostics.{commissioning_notice,authority,certified_band}` and appends the `CommissioningNotice: … engine will run` warning. Certified band (adapter default) copies MELTS: SiO2 ∈ [30, 80] wt%, T ∈ [1073.15, 1700] K (`data/engine_commissioning.yaml` `vaporock` row).
