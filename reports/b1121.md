# b-1121: preserve row-owned point pressure in identity

## Finding and implementation

The merge is in `simulator/battery/migrate.py` at the parent-row handling for
`PHASE_ROW_QUANTITIES`. Blame and `git log -L` point to commit `7e0508bb5`
(`feat: store printed phase compositions, distribution ratios, and S/Cl
capacities`, 2026-10-21). Its comment gives the reason: parent rows did not
otherwise ingest `point_conditions`; phase quantities needed the printed
atmosphere and `printed_sum` on every emitted observation. The history does
not name parent-over-row precedence as the reason for the gate.

The parent gate remains. Scalar non-phase observations now take their own
`values.point_conditions`, and an exploded non-phase observation promotes a
pressure only from that child row's own explicit condition when its identity
profile requires `total_pressure_Pa`. Parent conditions remain defaults in
`point_conditions`; a child condition replaces the same key and alone supplies
the child's pressure identity. Unknown printed states remain unknown. The
phase-row merge and its `printed_sum` handling are unchanged.

## Regression and mutation evidence

- Non-phase child row: `partial_molar_enthalpy`, parent pressure 100000 Pa,
  child pressure 80000 Pa. Identity gets 80000 Pa and the child locator.
- Parent-only values condition on a non-phase series: pressure is not copied
  into the child condition or its identity. A parent observation condition is
  retained as context, but does not become the child's identity pressure.
- Scalar non-phase observation: its own `values.point_conditions` pressure is
  promoted to identity.
- Phase-row migration and pressure-to-scorer coverage passes unchanged.
- Mutation: removing child-owned pressure promotion makes the child identity
  assertion fail (unknown pressure). Promoting from merged conditions instead
  makes the parent-only assertion fail (100000 Pa appears in the child
  identity).
